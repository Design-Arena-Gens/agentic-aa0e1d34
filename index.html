Real Time Stancer

Real Time Stancer is a Custom Shaders Patch Lua app for Assetto Corsa that lets you tweak wheel stance in real-time during gameplay.

  • Per-wheel offset, track width, camber, and ride height
  • Live adjustments without reloading the car
  • Preset management with automatic JSON storage
  • Requires CSP 0.1.80+ with Lua scripting enabled

Download the mod package

Installation

  1. Ensure Custom Shaders Patch 0.1.80 or newer is installed with Lua apps enabled.
  2. Download the mod archive and unzip into your Assetto Corsa root folder.
  3. Launch Assetto Corsa, enable Real Time Stancer in the in-game app sidebar, and tune on the fly.

Presets are stored in content/apps/lua/RealTimeStancer/config_presets.json.
